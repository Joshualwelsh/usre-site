The Problem We Solve

The retirement risk most people don’t see coming

During your working years, a market downturn is a setback. In retirement, the same downturn can be permanent, because you’re no longer adding money in. You’re taking it out.

Market timing risk

A downturn right before or during retirement can permanently reduce the income your savings are able to provide, even if the market eventually recovers.

No time to wait it out

Unlike your working years, retirement often doesn’t give you the runway to simply wait for a recovery while still covering your bills.

The withdrawal trap

Taking income out of a portfolio during a down market, known as sequence-of-returns risk, can compound losses far faster than most people expect.
